Good to know

Your questions,
answered simply.

A few helpful answers before you make your next move.

Ask us a question ↗
How can my company submit a hiring requirement?

Create an employer account, then share the role, location, number of openings and a job description if you have one. Your dashboard keeps the request organized.

How do I register as a candidate?

Create a candidate account, complete your skills and experience profile, upload your resume, and explore available roles.

Can I track my applications and enquiries?

Yes. Employers can track submitted hiring requirements, while candidates can view their applications in their respective dashboards.

Are live jobs available on the website?

Published vacancies appear on the Jobs page when the Talent Bridge team adds them. Career categories are not advertised as active vacancies.
